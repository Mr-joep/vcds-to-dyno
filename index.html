    How to log a pull in VCDS
    1. Open 01-Engine → Advanced Measuring Values.
    2. Select only Engine speed and Engine torque. Fewer values means a faster sample rate and a smoother curve.
    3. Click Log and then Start.
    4. In 3rd gear, go to full throttle from about 1,500 rpm up to just below the limiter, then lift off.
    5. Stop the log and drop the .csv file here.

    Only do this somewhere it is safe and legal, like a closed road or a track.
